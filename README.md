# CCNA Handwritten Notes

I prepared these handwritten CCNA notes during the second year of my engineering studies. They are a personal study resource that I revisit whenever I need to revise networking concepts.

## Course reference

The notes follow the excellent CCNA course and playlist by Jeremy's IT Lab:

- [Jeremy's IT Lab YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@JeremysITLab)
- [Complete CCNA course playlist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8W9oMNSuwo&list=PLxbwE86jKRgMpuZuLBivzlM8s2Dk5lXBQ)

I highly recommend Jeremy's channel and this playlist to anyone learning CCNA. The explanations are clear, practical, and especially useful for building a strong foundation in networking.

## Contents

The PDFs are organized by study day. They cover key CCNA topics including subnetting, VLANs, STP, routing protocols, IPv6, ACLs, DHCP, DNS, SSH, NAT, QoS, and more.

> These are personal revision notes. For the complete lessons and demonstrations, please refer to Jeremy's IT Lab course linked above.
